# Announcing the Account App

Today we're excited to introduce the **Account App**, a new centralized place for managing your Origami Systems account.
The Account App is designed to make managing your account simpler, more secure, and consistent across all Origami Systems applications. Instead of handling account settings separately in each application, you'll now have one dedicated location to manage your identity and authentication preferences.

## A Better Way to Manage Your Account

The Account App brings together everything related to your account, including:

- Account information
- Password management
- Password reset requests
- Passwordless sign-in
- Security settings
- Authentication methods

As we continue to expand the platform, new security features and authentication options will be added directly to the Account App.

## Built for the Future

The Account App is the foundation for future authentication features across the Origami Systems platform. As new applications are introduced, you'll continue using the same account, the same security settings, and the same authentication methods everywhere.
This also allows us to introduce new authentication technologies over time while keeping the experience familiar and consistent.

## Available Now

The Account App is now available for all users. If you already have an Origami Systems account, you can begin managing your account settings immediately.
Visit [account.origamisystems.org](https://account.origamisystems.org) to explore the new Account App, and stay tuned for additional security and account management features in future updates.
