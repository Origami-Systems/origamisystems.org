# Announcing The Signups App

Today we're introducing The Signups App, a new way to organize events, coordinate volunteers, and manage signups with Origami Systems.

Whether you're organizing a school event, coordinating volunteers, or planning an activity, The Signups App makes it easy to create signup forms and keep everything organized in one place.

## Create signups for anything

The Signups App gives you the tools to create and manage signup forms for a variety of activities.

With The Signups App, you can:

- Create customized signup forms with titles, descriptions, and locations
- Organize signup opportunities into sections and slots
- Set capacity limits for individual slots
- Add dates and times to signup opportunities
- Allow participants to join multiple slots
- Enable waitlists when slots fill up
- Share signup forms with a public link
- Track participants and available spots

Whether you need five volunteers for an event or dozens of people across multiple activities, The Signups App helps you stay organized.

## Keep track of everything

Managing a signup shouldn't mean manually keeping track of participants or available spots.

The Signups App includes a dashboard that gives organizers an overview of their forms, including:

- Total signups and remaining availability
- Slots that still need participants
- Upcoming signup opportunities
- Waitlisted participants
- Recent signup activity
- Participant search and management

You can also export signup information to a CSV file, making it easy to work with your data outside The Signups App.

## Easy for participants

Signing up should be just as simple as creating a form.

Participants can open a shared signup link, view available opportunities, and select the slots that work for them.

They can also cancel their signup if their plans change, helping organizers keep availability up to date.

## Part of the Origami Systems Stack

The Signups App is part of the growing Origami Systems Stack, a collection of applications designed to make everyday tasks easier.

With an Origami Systems account, you can access The Signups App alongside other Origami Systems applications using the same identity system.

The Signups App is available now!
