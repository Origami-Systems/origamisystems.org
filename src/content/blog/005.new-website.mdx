The Origami Systems website has a new design. It now does a better job of explaining what we make and why, and it is easier to find the app you are looking for.

## Why we redesigned it

The old site was mostly about schools. Schools are where Origami Systems started, but our apps now work for any group that needs to organize people and keep them informed, including clubs, teams, and other organizations.

We also wanted the site to say plainly why Origami Systems exists: to make technology work for people, instead of making people work around technology.

## What's new

- **A new home page.** The home page covers the three principles behind every product (simplicity, security, and reliability), how our apps connect through one account, and who they are for.
- **An About page.** Read why we started Origami Systems and what we care about when we build something.
- **A redesigned Products page.** Each app now has its main features, a link to open it, and a link to learn more. The page also covers what comes with every app, like your Origami Systems account and The Help App.
- **A page for The Signups App.** [The Signups App page](/signups) shows what a signup looks like, what the app can do, and how to get started in three steps.
- **A page for schools.** [For Schools](/schools) explains how schools use our apps for house systems, news and events, volunteers, and conferences.
- **A better footer.** All of our apps are now listed in one place, alongside support links like status, FAQ, terms, and privacy.
