Until now, every Origami Systems account belonged to a single organization, such as a school or church, and that organization created it for you. You can now create a personal account yourself.

## Your account, across organizations

A personal account is yours. It uses your email address, and it can have access to more than one organization.

To join an organization, accept an invitation from its administrator using the email address the invitation was sent to. You'll see every organization you belong to on the Organizations page of the Account App. When you sign in and belong to more than one, you choose which organization to use.

## Create a personal account

1. Go to [account.origamisystems.org](https://account.origamisystems.org), switch to the personal tab, and choose **Create personal account**.
2. Enter your name, username, and email, then set a password.
3. Verify your email using the link we send you.

After you sign in, you can add a passkey so future sign-ins are faster.

## Delete your account

You can delete a personal account from the Account App. Deletion is scheduled for 7 days later. During that time you're signed out of other devices and can't use other apps, and you can choose **Keep My Account** to cancel. Organizations you belong to keep the work you created in them.

## Available now

Visit [account.origamisystems.org](https://account.origamisystems.org) to create a personal account.

Need help? The [Help App](https://help.origamisystems.org) has guides for your account, and a [contact form](https://help.origamisystems.org/contact) if you need more help.
