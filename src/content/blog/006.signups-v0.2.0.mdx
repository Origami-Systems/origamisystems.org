# The Signups App v0.2.0

Version 0.2.0 of The Signups App is out. It moves notification preferences to your account and makes forms and dashboards easier to use.

## Notification preferences in one place

Notification preferences used to live inside each signup form, so you had to set them again for every form you joined. They now apply to your whole account. Open the account menu in the top corner and choose **Notification preferences** to set them once.

You can turn email and push notifications on or off, and choose which updates you get for the signups you join:

- Confirm when I sign up
- Confirm when I cancel
- Tell me when I move off the waitlist
- Tell me when a slot I joined changes

Each one can be sent by email, push, or both.

## Other improvements

- If a signup or dashboard fails to load, you'll see what went wrong and a **Try again** button.
- Form settings now explain each option. For example, **Allow waitlists** tells you that people join a first-come waitlist when a slot fills.
- The signup deadline setting tells you what happens when you leave it blank.
- My Signups has a search box and filters for finding a signup you joined.
- The embed popup has a field for copying the embed code.
- Form details on the dashboard are easier to scan.
