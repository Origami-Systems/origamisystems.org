# Introducing the Stack Button

The Stack button gives you one menu for getting around the Origami Systems Stack. Look for the layered icon in the title bar of any of our Apps.

## What's in the menu

From the Stack button you can:

- See which account and organization you're using
- Open any app you have access to
- Switch organizations, if your personal account belongs to more than one
- Install the app on your device, when your browser supports it
- Open settings
- Visit [Help & Support](https://help.origamisystems.org)
- Sign out

The apps list only shows apps you have access to, so it stays short.

## Switching organizations

If your personal account has access to more than one organization, the Stack button lists them. Pick one and the app reloads with that organization's data. If your account belongs to a single organization, the Stack button shows it but doesn't offer a switch.

## Coming to more apps

The Stack button will appear in more Origami Systems apps as they're updated.
